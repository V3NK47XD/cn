----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRACTICAL 14: PACKET SNIFFING USING RAW SOCKETS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AIM:
  To capture and inspect network traffic headers using raw sockets.

ALGORITHM:
  1. Create a raw socket with necessary administrative privileges.
  2. Bind socket to specific physical network adapter if required by the operating system.
  3. Continuously capture frames from interface; unpack Ethernet, IP, and transport layer headers.
  4. Extract and display header fields: Source IP, Destination IP, Protocol, and Payload Length.
  5. Terminate packet capture loop and close the raw socket handle.

RESULT:
  Raw-socket packet capture and protocol header decoding were implemented.
