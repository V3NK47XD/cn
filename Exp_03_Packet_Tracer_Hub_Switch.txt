----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRACTICAL 3: CISCO PACKET TRACER AND HUB/SWITCH
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AIM:
  To learn the Cisco Packet Tracer environment and compare the behavior of hubs versus switches.

PROCEDURE:
  1. Open Cisco Packet Tracer and explore the interface:
     - Menu Bar & Main Toolbar
     - Logical/Physical Workspace
     - Device Selection Box & Connections Box
     - Realtime & Simulation Mode Controls
  2. Place four PCs and one network hub on the workspace.
  3. Connect all four PCs to the hub using copper straight-through cables.
  4. Assign each PC a unique IP address within the exact same subnet mask.
  5. Replicate the topology replacing the hub with a network switch.
  6. Send a Protocol Data Unit (PDU) between two PCs in Simulation mode and observe forwarding.

COMPARISON OBSERVATIONS:
+-------------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------+
|Device|Forwarding Method|Operation & Collision Behavior|
+-------------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------+
|Hub|Broadcast (Repeats)|Repeats incoming signals to all ports; shared collision dom|
|Switch|Unicast (Filtering)|Reads MAC table; forwards frames only to the target port|
+-------------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------+

RESULT:
  Packet Tracer was used to build and simulate networks; hub and switch behavior was compared.
